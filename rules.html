📜 Meeting Rules & Information

  • Proper care of the chessboards and pieces must be treated as our top priority. Some of the boards are not the school's and are supplied by teachers who personally own them.
  • Absolutely NO swearing or inappropriate insults of any kind.
  • Trash talk is allowed, but it should not be improper.
  • Feel free to invite friends who want to join but are not currently in Google Classroom.
  • Upper Elementary students (Grades 4–5) will be allowed if they behave appropriately.
  • We will meet in MS 407 around once or twice a month to talk about different aspects of chess, as well as hold our quarterly chess tournament. Otherwise, we will meet in the MS Commons.
  • Snacks may or may not be given depending on the circumstance. Snacks will be given to game and tournament winners, as well as some of the first people who arrive at the meeting. We do not want greasy fingers touching expensive chessboards!

⚠️ Rule Violations

Failure to follow these rules will result in consequences matching the severity of the violation, possibly including temporary or permanent bans from the club.

🧩 Puzzle Information

  • Weekly puzzles will be posted at 3:30 PM on meeting days.
  • Monthly puzzles will be posted at a random day and time of the month, so make sure to check Google Classroom and Gmail regularly.
  • People who finish puzzles at similar times may or may not both receive 1st place prizes, depending on the circumstance.
