Meeting Rules

Keep Chess Club welcoming, fair, and fun.

01

Take care of the equipment

Proper care of the chessboards and pieces must be treated as our top priority. Some boards are not the school's and are supplied by teachers who personally own them.

02

Keep it respectful

Absolutely no swearing or inappropriate insults of any kind.

03

Trash talk is okay — keep it appropriate

Friendly trash talk is allowed, but it should not be improper.

04

Friends are welcome

Feel free to invite friends who want to join but are not currently in Google Classroom.

05

Upper Elementary may attend

Upper Elementary students in Grades 4–5 will be allowed if they behave appropriately.

06

Know where we're meeting

We will meet in MS 407 around once or twice a month to talk about different aspects of chess and hold our quarterly chess tournament. Otherwise, we will meet in the MS Commons.

07

Snacks

Snacks may or may not be given depending on the circumstance. Snacks will be given to game and tournament winners, as well as some of the first people who arrive at the meeting. We do not want greasy fingers touching expensive chessboards!

⚠️

Rule Violations

Failure to follow these rules will result in consequences matching the severity of the violation, possibly including temporary or permanent bans from the club.

Warning → Restriction → Possible Ban

How Puzzles Work

Optional challenges that give you another way to earn points.

📅

Weekly Puzzles

Posted at 3:30 PM – 5:00 PM on meeting days.

🗓️

Monthly Puzzles

Posted at a random day and time of the month. Check Google Classroom and Gmail regularly.

🏆

Ties & Prizes

People who finish puzzles at similar times may both receive 1st place prizes depending on the circumstance.

💡

Completely Optional

Puzzles are optional and are there for an extra challenge. You can submit them even if you're not competing for a prize.
