Think. Plan. Checkmate.

We aim to create a welcoming environment where students of all skill levels can learn, compete, and develop their chess abilities.

Our Objectives

Three areas that guide what we do.

🧠

Learning

Build stronger chess knowledge and develop better decision-making skills.

✓ Learn chess fundamentals and rules
✓ Improve opening, tactical, and endgame knowledge
✓ Develop strategic thinking and decision-making
✓ Analyze games to improve
🏆

Competition

Create opportunities for members to compete, improve, and challenge themselves.

✓ Organize internal tournaments
✓ Create a fair ranking system
✓ Encourage friendly competition
✓ Prepare members for competitions
🤝

Community

Build a welcoming chess community where everyone can learn, play, and improve together.

✓ Welcome players of all skill levels
✓ Promote respect, fair play, and sportsmanship
✓ Encourage teamwork and positive communication
✓ Create a place for students to enjoy chess together
♟
