♟ Our Mission

The Chess Club aims to create a welcoming environment where students of all skill levels can learn, compete, and develop their chess abilities.

🧠 Learning Objectives

• Learn chess fundamentals and rules
• Improve opening, tactical, and endgame knowledge
• Develop strategic thinking and decision-making skills
• Analyze games to improve

🏆 Competitive Objectives

• Organize internal tournaments
• Create a fair ranking system
• Encourage friendly competition
• Prepare members for competitions

🤝 Community Objectives

• Welcome players of all skill levels
• Encourage teamwork and sportsmanship
• Create a place for students to enjoy chess together
